---
title: Onboarding
description: A walkthrough of the 3-step setup flow that configures your study plan before you reach the dashboard.
---

When you sign in for the first time, LC Tracker runs a short onboarding flow to configure your study plan. The three steps take about two minutes and can all be changed later from **Settings**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect LeetCode and pick a learning mode">
    ## LeetCode username (optional)

    Enter your LeetCode username and click **Connect** to let LC Tracker fetch your recent accepted submissions. Matched problems are auto-marked as solved in your Problem Library so you are not re-assigned problems you have already done.

    <Note>
      LeetCode's public API only exposes your most recent accepted submissions. Problems you solved a long time ago may not appear. You can mark older solves manually from the Problem Library at any time.
    </Note>

    You can skip this step and add your username later under **Settings → LeetCode sync**.

    ## Learning mode

    Choose how LC Tracker sequences new problems for you:

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="What is Sprint mode?">
        Sprint mode focuses you on one problem category at a time (for example, Two Pointers or Binary Search) before moving on to the next. You work through a structured sprint of problems in that category, then advance. This mirrors the NeetCode-style approach and is well suited to learners who want systematic, pattern-by-pattern coverage.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="What is Random mode?">
        Random mode mixes problems across all categories in each session. You will not be locked into a single category sprint. This is useful if you want broader variety day-to-day or if you are already partway through your prep and want to maintain coverage across topics simultaneously.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Can I switch later?">
        Yes. Go to **Settings → Learning Strategy** to toggle between Sprint and Random at any time. Switching does not reset your progress.
      </Accordion>
    </AccordionGroup>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set skill levels per category">
    LC Tracker uses your self-reported skill level for each problem category to calibrate which problems to surface first and how aggressively to introduce new material.

    For each category (for example, Arrays, Sliding Window, Trees, Dynamic Programming), choose one of three levels:

    | Level | Meaning |
    |---|---|
    | **Not familiar** | You have little to no experience with this pattern. LC Tracker will start from the easiest problems and build up gradually. |
    | **Some exposure** | You have seen or attempted problems in this category but are not confident. LC Tracker will introduce problems at a moderate pace. |
    | **Comfortable** | You can solve most problems in this category reliably. LC Tracker will skip easy introductory problems and prioritize review and harder variants. |

    <Tip>
      Be honest with your skill ratings. Overrating yourself leads to problems that are too hard too soon; underrating means you spend time on problems you have already mastered. You can adjust skill levels at any time in **Settings → Skill levels**.
    </Tip>

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="What if I skip a category?">
        Any category you leave unrated defaults to **Not familiar**, which is the safest starting point. LC Tracker will introduce the category from the beginning.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="How do skill levels affect the daily plan?">
        Skill levels influence which problems are queued as "new" each day. A category rated Comfortable will contribute fewer new problems (since you are assumed to need less ramp-up time), while Not familiar categories get a slower, more deliberate introduction. Spaced-repetition reviews are scheduled independently of skill level once a problem has been solved.
      </Accordion>
    </AccordionGroup>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Launch">
    The final step shows a summary of your choices — learning mode and LeetCode connection status — along with a few quick tips before you launch.

    At launch, LC Tracker commits your settings and takes you to the Dashboard with your first Daily Plan ready.

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="What are the default study schedule settings?">
        If you skip detailed schedule configuration during onboarding, LC Tracker uses these defaults:

        - **Weekday budget**: 60 minutes per day
        - **Weekend budget**: 120 minutes per day
        - **Rest day**: Sunday (day index 0)
        - **Blackout dates**: none

        You can customize all of these under **Settings → Study schedule**.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="What other settings use defaults?">
        The following settings are not part of onboarding and start from sensible defaults that you can adjust in Settings at any time:

        - **Target company tier**: Mixed (covers FAANG, fintech, and general companies)
        - **Interview type**: Internship
        - **Preferred language**: Python
        - **Spaced-repetition aggressiveness**: Relaxed (longer intervals between reviews)
        - **Sprint settings**: Strict mode on, standard sprint length
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="What counts as a daily streak?">
        Your streak increments when you complete at least one problem or review session on a given calendar day. You get one grace day per week — if you miss a day, the streak is preserved as long as you have not already used your grace day that week.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Can I export my data?">
        Yes. Go to **Settings** and use the **Export JSON** option to download a full backup of your progress, history, and settings at any time.
      </Accordion>
    </AccordionGroup>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Re-running onboarding

You can revisit and change any onboarding setting at any time — you do not need to re-run the full flow. All individual settings are available in the **Settings** tab:

- LeetCode username: **Settings → LeetCode sync**
- Learning mode: **Settings → Learning Strategy**
- Skill levels: **Settings → Skill levels**
- Study schedule: **Settings → Study schedule**
- Target company, interview type, language, and more: **Settings → Plan Customization**
