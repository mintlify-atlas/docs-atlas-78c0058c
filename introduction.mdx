LC Tracker is a full-stack web app built for anyone who takes LeetCode prep seriously. Instead of picking random problems each day, LC Tracker gives you a daily plan, tracks your confidence over time with spaced repetition, and shows you exactly where you stand with a readiness score.

Sign in at [lc-tracker.app](https://lc-tracker.app) to get started. Your progress syncs across all your devices.

## What LC Tracker does

LC Tracker solves two core problems that make self-study hard:

- **What should I work on today?** — The daily plan picks the right new problem and review queue for you, respecting your available time.
- **Will I remember what I learned?** — Spaced repetition schedules each problem for re-review at the right time, so your retention compounds instead of decaying.

## Core workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [lc-tracker.app](https://lc-tracker.app) with your Google account. Your data syncs across devices from day one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete onboarding">
    Tell LC Tracker your target company tier, interview type, preferred language, and how much time you have each day. This shapes your entire plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow your daily plan">
    Each day the dashboard shows you exactly which problem to solve and which problems to review — no decision fatigue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rate every session">
    After each problem, rate your confidence 1–3. This drives the spaced repetition scheduler and your readiness score.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  LC Tracker covers the NeetCode 75 in Phase 1 and expands to the NeetCode 150 in Phase 2. You advance automatically when you complete Phase 1 with sufficient confidence.
</Note>
