LC Tracker supports two learning modes: **Sprint Mode** and **Random Mode**. You choose your mode in Settings.

## Sprint Mode vs Random Mode

| Mode | How new problems are selected |
|------|-------------------------------|
| **Sprint Mode** | One category at a time; you advance only after passing a Sprint Check |
| **Random Mode** | Next unsolved problem from the full NeetCode 75 or 150 list |

Sprint Mode is the default and recommended approach. It builds deep pattern recognition before moving on, rather than scattering your attention across all categories at once.

## Phase 1 and Phase 2

Sprints are organized into two phases:

- **Phase 1** covers the NeetCode 75 problem set across 18 categories
- **Phase 2** covers the extended NeetCode 150 set

**Phase 1 categories (in order):** Arrays & Hashing, Two Pointers, Sliding Window, Stack, Binary Search, Linked List, Trees, Tries, Heap/Priority Queue, Backtracking, Graphs, Advanced Graphs, 1-D DP, 2-D DP, Greedy, Intervals, Math & Geometry, Bit Manipulation

<Note>
  The Phase 1 milestone triggers when you've solved all 75 NeetCode 75 problems with an average confidence rating of 2.0 or higher. Reaching it unlocks Phase 2 and opens the NeetCode 150 problem set.
</Note>

## Sprint lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Active sprint">
    You work through problems in the current sprint category. The sidebar shows your sprint day (e.g., Day 3 of 7) and overall progress. New problems on the Daily Plan are drawn exclusively from the active category.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sprint Check (retrospective)">
    When your sprint days expire, the Daily Plan enters retrospective mode. Today's Focus is replaced by a **Sprint Check** — a single timed problem from your sprint category, solved mock-interview style.

    The time limit is set to 1.5× your average solve time for that category.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pass or extend">
    After the Sprint Check, you rate your attempt:
    - **Rating 2 or 3 (pass)** — you advance to the next category
    - **Rating 1 or timeout (fail)** — the sprint extends by 2 days and you try again
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sprint complete">
    Once you pass the Sprint Check, that category is marked complete and the next sprint begins automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sprint settings

You can tune sprint behavior in Settings under **Sprint Settings**:

| Setting | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Strict Mode** | When enabled, you cannot manually skip to the next category until you pass the Sprint Check |
| **Length Multiplier** | Scales the base sprint length (1.0 = default; 0.75 = shorter; 1.25 = longer) |
| **Target Days** | Override sprint length to a fixed number of days across all categories |

Base sprint length depends on your skill level for that category (set during onboarding): 7 days for Not Familiar, 4 days for Some Exposure, 2 days for Comfortable.

## Changing the sprint category manually

If you want to jump to a different category — for example, because you already know Arrays & Hashing well — you can use the dropdown in the Sprint card on the Dashboard sidebar. It lists all Phase 1 and Phase 2 categories. Selecting a new category immediately starts a sprint for that category.
