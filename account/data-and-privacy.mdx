## What data is stored

LC Tracker stores the following data on your behalf to power your study plan, spaced-repetition schedule, and analytics.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Problem progress">
    For each problem you interact with, LC Tracker stores:

    - `firstSolvedAt` — when you first marked the problem as solved
    - `lastReviewedAt` — the date of your most recent review session
    - `nextReviewAt` — when the problem is next scheduled for review (calculated by the spaced-repetition algorithm)
    - `reviewCount` — how many times you've reviewed the problem
    - `history` — a log of past review ratings
    - `retired` — whether you've removed the problem from future review cycles
    - `notes` — any personal notes you've written on the problem
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Session timings">
    Each practice or review session records:

    - Elapsed time in seconds
    - The problem category
    - Your difficulty rating for the session
    - The session type (new problem, review, mock interview, etc.)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sprint state and history">
    If you use Sprint Mode, LC Tracker tracks your current sprint category, progress within that sprint, and a history of completed sprints including their start and end dates.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Activity log">
    Daily counts of problems solved and problems reviewed, used to power your streaks, heatmap, and pacing analytics.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Settings">
    Your full app configuration is stored per account, including:

    - Study schedule (weekday/weekend targets, rest day, blackout dates)
    - Target company tier and interview type
    - Learning mode (Sprint or Random) and sprint settings
    - Spaced-repetition aggressiveness
    - Preferred programming language
    - Target interview events
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Syntax card progress">
    For each syntax reference card, LC Tracker stores your confidence rating and the number of times you've reviewed it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="LeetCode username">
    If you've connected your LeetCode account, your LeetCode username is stored so LC Tracker can re-sync your submission history on demand.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Exporting your data

You can download a full copy of your data at any time from **Settings → Export Backup**. Clicking **Export Data** generates a JSON file named `lc-tracker-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json` and saves it to your downloads folder.

The export includes:

- All problem progress records
- Session timings
- Activity log
- Sprint state and sprint history
- All app settings

This file is a complete snapshot of your account data and can be used as an offline backup.

## Sending feedback

The **Feedback** button in the app opens a form where you can send bug reports, feature requests, or general comments directly to the LC Tracker team.

To submit feedback:

1. Click **Feedback** in the app.
2. Select the type: **Feature Request**, **Bug Report**, or **General Feedback**.
3. Write your message.
4. Optionally attach a screenshot (JPG, PNG, or WebP, up to 5 MB).
5. Click **Send Feedback**.

You'll see a confirmation message once your feedback is submitted.
