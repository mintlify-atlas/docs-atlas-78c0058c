---
title: Authentication
description: How to create an account, sign in, and manage your LC Tracker account.
---

LC Tracker uses Google Sign-In to create and manage your account. There are no passwords to set up — just sign in with your existing Google account.

## Creating an account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the sign-in page">
    Visit [lc-tracker.app](https://lc-tracker.app) and click **Sign In** to reach the login screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue with Google">
    Click **Continue with Google**. You'll be redirected to Google to choose or confirm your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the app">
    LC Tracker requests only your name and email address — nothing else from your Google account. Review the permissions and click **Allow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're in">
    After authorizing, you'll be redirected back to your LC Tracker dashboard. Your account is created automatically on first sign-in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your progress, settings, and problem history are all tied to your account and sync across devices automatically. Sign in on any browser or device to pick up exactly where you left off.
</Note>

## Signing back in

Return to [lc-tracker.app](https://lc-tracker.app), click **Sign In**, and choose the same Google account you used to register. You don't need to do anything special — LC Tracker recognizes your account and takes you straight to your dashboard.

## Signing out

Click your profile or account menu in the app and select **Sign Out**. Your data remains saved to your account and will be waiting when you sign back in.

## Deleting your account

To delete your account and all associated data, contact the LC Tracker team at [alesio1610@gmail.com](mailto:alesio1610@gmail.com) with a deletion request. Your data will be removed from the system.

You can also revoke LC Tracker's access to your Google account independently by visiting your [Google Account permissions page](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) and removing LC Tracker. Note that revoking access prevents sign-in but does not automatically delete any data already stored.
